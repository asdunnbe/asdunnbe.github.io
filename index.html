Andrea Dunn Beltran

Hi! I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at UNC Chapel Hill, researching 3D computer vision under the guidance of Professor Roni Sengupta

My research centers on physics-informed computer vision for medicine. Currently, I focus on reliable 3D/4D reconstruction, tracking, and simulation specifically in endoscopy, including near-field lighting and deformable reconstruction of airway and colon data.

Previously, I earned my B.S. in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics at UNC Chapel Hill, was a visiting undergraduate at Rice University and USC, and later a visiting graduate student at Harvard Medical School.

Outside the lab, I enjoy traveling, nature, learning the bass guitar, and photography!
